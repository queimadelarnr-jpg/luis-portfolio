I'm Luis, a multidisciplinary creative and web designer based in Portugal. My background has taken me through tattooing, photography, product design, and running my own business — experiences that have shaped the way I approach creative work today. Working across different disciplines taught me to think both visually and practically: to pay attention to detail, understand how things are made, communicate with people, and take an idea from the first sketch all the way to the finished result. Today, I bring that same mindset to web design. I'm interested in creating digital experiences where typography, imagery, motion, and interaction all work together to give a project its own character. I like making things that feel considered, purposeful, and a little different. I am currently available for freelance work.
